### 🚀 About Me

I'm a Software Engineer working across AI and full-stack, currently **AI Engineer & Tech Lead at dissolveX**.

Most of what I build starts from a stubborn constraint: run it offline, ship it with no dependencies, keep it fast. That has produced an assistant that reasons entirely on your own machine, a full game in 94 KB of vanilla JavaScript with no build step, and an ambient mixer with no backend to fall over.

- 🔭 **Currently:** AI Engineer & Tech Lead at **dissolveX** — AI agents, workflow automation, LLM and RAG integration
- 🧪 **Also building:** expanding **Helio**, an offline AI holographic assistant
- 🎓 **Studying:** B.Tech CSE (AI & ML — IBM) at JECRC University, Jaipur · CGPA 8.3 · 2026
